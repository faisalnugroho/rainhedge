Three steps, settled by consensus

The covered windows are historical: the weather is already final when you open cover. That is the point — the chain measures byte-pinned records, so nobody can reinterpret the weather afterwards.

01

Underwrite

Pick a location and 1–4 historical weekly windows. The dApp fetches each week's canonical Open-Meteo Archive record from the project's commit-pinned record catalog (portable to the validators' allowed hosts), hashes it, and commits the sha256 pins on-chain with your trigger and payout.

02

Fund

Pay the premium as a real value transfer into the contract escrow. Coverage, trigger and payout percentage were locked at step 01 — the premium is the only thing that moves.

03

Resolve

After the challenge window, validators re-fetch the pinned URLs, re-verify every sha256, re-measure totals in exact integer math, and witness the records. Every window under trigger = PAYOUT; anything else fails closed and your premium stays claimable.
